Why Dynasty Surfaces

Factory Direct.
Fully Specified.
No Guesswork.

Dynasty Surfaces LLC is a New Jersey company sourcing porcelain, ceramic and GVT tile from our manufacturing partner in Morbi, Gujarat. We work directly with the plant, so you buy at the export price rather than through a trading house.

We publish our full size range, box data and container loading figures below, because a serious buyer should be able to work out their own order before they ever pick up the phone. Pricing is quoted FOB Mundra and fully costed: material, pallet packing, inland transport to port and export clearance are all included.

On duty, we deal in facts rather than claims. Indian porcelain enters the US under HTS heading 6907 and carries tariffs and countervailing duties that move with trade policy. We will walk through the current rates with you and your customs broker before you commit to a container, so there are no surprises at the port.

Technical Performance

Tested Against
ISO 13006 and EN 14411

Our manufacturing partner holds ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certification and publishes performance data against the ISO 13006 and EN 14411 standards. Where our figures exceed what the standard requires, we have shown both so you can judge for yourself. Full test documentation is available on request.

< 0.05%
Water Absorption
The porcelain threshold under ANSI A137.1 is 0.5 percent. Our tile tests at a tenth of that, which places it firmly in the fully vitrified category. Tested to ISO 10545-3.
2,000 N
Breaking Strength
The standard calls for a minimum of 1,300 N. Our minimum is 2,000 N, roughly 54 percent above requirement. Tested to ISO 10545-4.
